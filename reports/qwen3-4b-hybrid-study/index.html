Official workflow lineage

The implementation follows NVIDIA ModelOpt's examples/dataset workflow: make_nemotron_ptv2_dataset.py, make_nemotron_ptv3_dataset.py, nemotron_ptv3_datasets.yaml, conversation_utils.py, and query.py. Extensions make thinking mode explicit, sample by assistant-token quota instead of lexical row prefixes, preserve tool schemas and call/result IDs, and separate target synthesis from trace replay.

Decision gate

Selection uses acceptance length/rate and end-to-end speed at concurrency 1/8/32, split by SPEED category, math/code, held-out SWE/tool traces, and context buckets ≤4K, 4–16K, and 16–32K. Correctness scores must remain target-equivalent because speculative decoding is lossless. Arm C/D must improve SWE acceptance without a material math or general-category regression.
